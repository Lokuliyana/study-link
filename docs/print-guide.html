Study Link Counselor OS: System Requirement & Operational Guide

Rapid 3-Minute Triage Engine + 10-Country Rules Matrix + Counselor CRM Pipeline

1. Executive Summary & Operating Principles

This contract governs the implementation of the Study Link Counselor OS. It is designed for front-line educational counselors handling high-velocity inquiry calls. In a 3-minute phone interaction, the system evaluates candidate eligibility against 10 destination countries, highlights relevant hooks, and records the lead into an appointment conversion pipeline.

Non-Negotiable System Constraint: The frontend is read-heavy. All 10 country rules, qualifications, and pitch scripts reside in deterministic client-side JSON configurations. Search and eligibility calculation must resolve in under 50 milliseconds without external API delays.

2. 3-Minute Call Flow Architecture

[0:00 - 0:45] TRIAGE (Highest Qual, Gap Years, English Test Status) │ ▼ [0:46 - 2:00] MATCH (Latvia, Malta, UK, Finland, Germany, Lithuania, Ireland, Cyprus, Malaysia, Dubai) │ ▼ [2:01 - 3:00] APPOINTMENT HOOK ("Deadlines close in 2 weeks; bring certificates to Nugegoda tomorrow.") │ ▼ [3:01 - 3:30] CRM AUTO-LOG (WhatsApp Number, Target Intake, Auto-WhatsApp Confirmation)

4. Lead CRM Pipeline & Operational Tracking

Every lead captured through social inquiries or direct phone calls flows through 6 standardized stages:

Stage Definition & Counselor Objective System Action
1. New Lead Uncontacted inquiry from Facebook, TikTok, or Website. Highlights lead in red if uncalled after 15 minutes.
2. Triaged 3-minute qualification call completed; country fit identified. Tags matched country and attaches customized script hook.
3. Appointment Set Candidate committed to visit Nugegoda office or attend Zoom session. Dispatches WhatsApp confirmation with location pin & checklist.
4. File Opened Student visited office, provided passport/marksheets, paid advance. Creates document verification checklist (AIC, VFS, etc.).
5. Visa Lodged Application submitted to university and visa lodged at VFS/Embassy. Sets interview preparation reminders and embassy trackers.
6. Closed / Enrolled Visa approved, tuition finalized, and flight details scheduled. Marks deal as won and updates intake conversion analytics.

6. Definition of Done (Quality Gate)

  • Sub-50ms Calculation: Filtering by age, gap, and qualifications dynamically highlights eligible countries without full-page reloads.
  • One-Click WhatsApp Integration: Clicking the student phone number triggers wa.me/94... with a pre-filled appointment reminder.
  • Zero External Database Dependency: Leads and updates persist reliably to localStorage or data/leads.json for lightweight, maintenance-free operations.
